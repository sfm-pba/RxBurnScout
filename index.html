About RxBurnScout

RxBurnScout is a free, open-source prescribed burn weather planning tool for landowners and land managers. It compares hourly National Weather Service (NWS) forecasts with the conditions you specify, then highlights weather forecast windows that meet those conditions.

How it works

  1. Choose a forecast area. Pan and zoom the map to your site, and select a square from NOAA's 2.5-km forecast grid.
  2. Set your burn-unit criteria. Choose acceptable ranges for wind speed, relative humidity, temperature, and specify the minimum number of consecutive hours required.
  3. Analyze the NWS hourly forecast. RxBurnScout evaluates the forecast hour-by-hour against your criteria. A potential burn window is identified only when all criteria pass your required number of consecutive hours.
  4. Review the forecast. Scan the seven-day outlook for potential burn days, then inspect the details. Refresh the forecast whenever you want updated NWS information.

Significant Details

  • Multiple burn units: Save different forecast areas and weather criteria for multiple units.
  • Browser-based access: Use the application on a desktop, tablet, or phone without installing software or creating an account.

Your data and privacy

By default, your burn units and settings are saved locally in your browser. You can export your units as a JSON backup file.

The Settings tab offers optional synchronization through a server that you choose and configure. Connecting a server is not required to use RxBurnScout. The app makes internet requests to the NWS for forecasts and to OpenStreetMap, and must share the location you choose in order for the app to function.

Forecasts are fetched when you open an analysis or choose to refresh it. This app does not currently provide unattended forecast monitoring or automatic notifications.

Information sources

  • Weather data comes from the public National Weather Service API.
  • The map is provided by OpenStreetMap.org.
  • The grid overlay (on the map) is generated from NOAA's continental U.S. NDFD grid.

Open-source project

RxBurnScout is available for anyone to use, inspect, improve, and share under its open-source license. Find the application code, license, and project history in the RxBurnScout GitHub repository.

RxBurnScout is a True North Seed Project initiative, hosted by the Saint Francois Mountains Prescribed Burn Association.

WHAT IS A BURN WINDOW?A burn window is a period when the forecast meets all of your specified weather criteria.

This setting determines the minimum number of consecutive hours those conditions must be met for RxBurnScout to identify a qualifying window.

For example, a setting of 4 hours means that all specified criteria must be satisfied for at least four consecutive forecast hours. If even one criterion fails during an hour, that hour does not count toward the required duration.

This identifies a potential forecast window; it does not guarantee that conditions on the ground are suitable or that a burn should proceed.
